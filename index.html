Hello You!  Yes you - reading this...

Let's change the world of mathematics together, and we can do it with Mathican: a strictly defined coded pure mathematical language. Curiously, such a thing does not currently exist.

There are many recognised universal conventions, not least the Arabic place value system we use for numbers. Mathican standardises the coded language swimming around in all that convention. Now the fun part - we define it, AND design it!

The design principles of Mathican are:

Clarity - understanding is front and centre

Connectivity - all Mathican fits together

Comfort - mathematics should be enjoyable
